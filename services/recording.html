The Session

Where records get made

A recording session at Salvation Studios is unlike any other. Whether you're tracking a full band in the Live Room, laying down vocals in our dedicated booth, or overdubbing strings in the isolation booths — every space has been designed by world-renowned acoustician John Flynn to give your recordings the best possible start. Our in-house engineers know this building intimately. They know every inch of the Neve 8068, every microphone in the locker, and every trick the acoustics of each room have to offer.

We work with artists at every stage — from pre-production planning and arrangement to final performance capture. Our role is to make the process as invisible as possible, so you can focus on what matters: the music.
