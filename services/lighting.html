Lighting & Production

Set the Atmosphere

State-of-the-art lighting throughout — infinitely configurable for any colour, intensity, and mood.

The System

Light designed for music

The lighting at Salvation Studios was designed alongside the acoustics — a thoughtful part of the creative environment rather than an afterthought. Every room features a fully programmable LED system, capable of producing any colour temperature, intensity, or dynamic effect. The right atmosphere makes a difference. Musicians perform better when they feel good. Sessions run deeper when the room feels right.

From the warm amber of a late-night session to the cool blue of a focused morning write — the lighting at Salvation adapts to whatever the music demands.

Full RGB Control

Infinite colour options — any colour temperature, any saturation, any mood. Programmable from the control room or by the studio manager.

RGB LED Full Spectrum

Zone Control

Independent lighting zones in every room — set a different atmosphere in the live room, the vocal booth, and the control room simultaneously.

Multi-Zone Independent Control

Video & Content

Our lighting system integrates with video production workflows for content creation sessions, live performance recordings, and social media shoots.
