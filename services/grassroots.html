Grassroots

Serious studio time.
More accessible rates.

Affordable recording rates for unsigned and emerging artists in Brighton.

Built for independent artists

A professional room shouldn’t feel out of reach.

We know professional studio time can sometimes feel out of reach when you’re building a project independently. However, our Grassroots scheme provides an affordable way for emerging and independent artists to access a world-class studio used by industry leading producers and globally renowned acts.

To get the ball rolling, just tell us some more about your project and we can talk you through the recording process and our affordable rates, as well as answering any questions you might have.
