Your session, your rules

Dry hire at Salvation Studios gives you full access to our world-class facilities without the overhead of in-house staff. Bring your own engineer, producer, or mix team and work exactly as you would in your own space — but with the acoustic excellence and equipment that only Salvation can offer. All rooms available for dry hire: the Live Room, Control Room, Vocal Booth, Isolation Booths, Amp Booth, and Writing Rooms.

Our dry hire rates are transparent and competitive. Access to the Neve 8068, ATC monitors, the full microphone locker, and the outboard collection is all included. No hidden fees.
