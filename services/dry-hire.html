Your Way

The studio is available for dry hire, with a studio assistant provided to help the session run smoothly. Ask about rooms, dates, equipment and terms.

Flexible Booking

Your session, your rules

Dry hire at Salvation Studios is available for artists, producers and teams who want to bring their own creative crew into the complex. The current site confirms that a studio assistant is provided with dry-hire sessions, so enquiries should include the rooms, dates, team size and equipment needs for confirmation.

Rates, equipment access and room availability should be confirmed directly with the studio before booking. The team can advise what is included for the planned session and what needs to be arranged in advance.
