The service

Built around the music

Give your music the final polish with professional mastering carried out in a dedicated mastering environment. We work with trusted mastering engineers using specialist equipment to ensure your track translates with clarity, consistency and impact.

What You Get

  • Professional mastering by experienced engineers
  • Dedicated mastering suite and monitoring environment
  • Optimisation for streaming and distribution
  • Final quality control before release
Workflow

A practical route through the studio.

We often offer preferential mastering rates when booked alongside mixing or recording sessions, allowing your project to move smoothly through to its final stage. Every project is different, and we're always happy to tailor the process to suit your needs.

Why Salvation?

Rooms designed for translation

Mastering is a critical step in the process. By working with dedicated mastering engineers and purpose-built rooms, your music receives the attention and precision it deserves before release.
