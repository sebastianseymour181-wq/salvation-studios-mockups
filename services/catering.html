Catering & Hospitality

Fuelled for Creativity

Long sessions demand great food. Our in-house catering keeps you fuelled and in the zone from start to finish.

The Service

Sessions that run deep

Great sessions have a rhythm — and catering is part of it. At Salvation Studios, our hospitality service is designed around the reality of long recording days. Fresh food prepared on-site, quality coffee and tea always available, and a kitchen stocked with whatever the session requires. We work to dietary requirements, allergies, and preferences — just let us know in advance.

For multi-day sessions, writing camps, and label bookings, we offer full catering packages covering breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks throughout the day. Our studio manager will discuss requirements with you during the booking process.

On-Site Preparation

Food prepared fresh on-site, not delivered. Every meal is made to order for the session — tailored to the team's preferences and dietary needs.

All Day Coverage

Breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks available throughout the day. Continuous coffee, tea, juices, and soft drinks. The kitchen never closes.
