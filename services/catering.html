Catering & Hospitality

Fuelled for Creativity

Long sessions need practical hospitality. Catering is arranged project by project, from simple hearty food to gourmet options.

The Service

Sessions that run deep

Great sessions have a rhythm, and hospitality is part of it. The current site describes catering as project-by-project, with locally prepared food ranging from simple, hearty options to gourmet quality. Share team size, session length and dietary needs in the enquiry so the team can confirm what is possible.

The studio can seat up to 20 people for catering. Multi-day sessions and writing camps should discuss food requirements during the booking process so arrangements are confirmed before arrival.
