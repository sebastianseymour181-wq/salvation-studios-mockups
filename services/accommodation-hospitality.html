Accommodation and Hospitality

Making Your Time at Salvation Easy.

Comfortable, practical support for artists, producers and teams working at the studio.

Enquire About Accommodation & Hospitality
Hospitality

Looked after from arrival.

Whether you're travelling across the country or staying with us for an extended recording project, we're here to make your time at Salvation as comfortable and stress-free as possible. Over the years, we've built strong relationships with local businesses, restaurants, and accommodation providers, creating a small community around the studio that helps us look after the artists, producers, and teams who work with us.

What We Can Arrange

Everything around the session.

  • Catering delivered directly to the studio from trusted local restaurants
  • Breakfast, lunch, and dinner options for recording sessions
  • Recommendations and bookings for local restaurants and cafés
  • Self-catering accommodation through trusted local Airbnb hosts
  • Accommodation options for solo artists, bands, and larger groups
  • Local knowledge and recommendations to help make your stay enjoyable

Tell us what you need.

Whether you need food delivered during a long session or accommodation for a multi-day project, we're happy to organise everything for you. Simply let us know what you need, and we'll take care of the rest.

We've worked hard to build great relationships with local businesses and accommodation providers, allowing us to offer reliable, convenient options that fit around your recording schedule.
