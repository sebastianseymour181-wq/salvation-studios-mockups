Mixing

Mix on the Neve

The finest mixing environment in the South of England — 1973 Neve 8068 in a John Flynn–designed room with ATC soffit-mounted monitors.

The Process

Where mixes come alive

Mixing at Salvation Studios means working in a room built from the ground up for critical listening. The ATC SCM150 monitors, soffit-mounted in a John Flynn–designed room, allow you to hear every element of your mix with total clarity and confidence. The 1973 Neve 8068 console adds the warmth, depth, and character that analogue summing brings to even the most modern recording.

Our mixing sessions are available with or without an in-house engineer. Bring your own mixer and use the room on a dry hire basis, or work with one of our resident engineers who know the desk and the room inside out.
