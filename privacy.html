Your information

Privacy Notice

How we handle personal information when you contact Salvation Studios.

Last updated: 20 July 2026

Who we are

Salvation Studios

Salvation Studios is responsible for the personal information described in this notice. You can contact us at info@salvationstudios.co.uk, by calling +44 333 444 5508, or at 79 North Street, Brighton and Hove, Brighton BN41 1DH.

Information we collect

Information you choose to send us

When you submit an enquiry or contact us directly, we may collect your name, artist or company name, email address, phone number, preferred dates, session requirements and anything else you include in your message.

How we use it

Responding to your enquiry

We use this information to respond, discuss availability, understand your project, prepare a quote and arrange services you ask us about. We process it where necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract and for our legitimate interest in managing genuine studio enquiries.

We do not use an enquiry to add you to marketing lists unless you separately choose to receive marketing.

Service providers

Who handles the information

We share information only where needed to operate our website and email, respond to you, provide requested services, or meet a legal obligation. Our website and enquiry delivery use service providers including Vercel, Resend and Microsoft 365. These providers may process information outside the UK under their applicable data-protection safeguards.

We do not sell your personal information.

Retention

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary to deal with your request, manage any resulting client relationship, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal, tax or accounting requirements. The exact period depends on whether your enquiry becomes a booking or ongoing working relationship.

Your rights

Access, correction and deletion

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask us for a copy of your personal information, ask us to correct or delete it, restrict or object to its use, or ask for information you supplied in a portable format. Contact info@salvationstudios.co.uk to make a request.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office if you are unhappy with how we use your information.
