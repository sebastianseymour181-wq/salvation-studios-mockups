A rare opportunity

Turn your ideas into a record that sounds sensational.

Here’s your chance to turn your original material from a collection of ideas into a professionally produced, radio-ready record at a world-class studio with Sean Genockey—at an affordable cost.

A London-based producer, engineer, mixer, songwriter and guitarist, Sean brings more than 30 years of experience and a reputation for doing things a little differently. Best known for alternative and rock, his production credits include The Black Crowes, Walt Disco, Reuben, SNAYX, The Magpie Salute, Shame and NewDad.

His work reaches well beyond one sound, with artists including Scott Matthews, Barns Courtney, Orla Gartland, Tom McRae and Jasmine Rodgers. As an engineer and mixer, he has worked with The Who, Suede, Manic Street Preachers, Kula Shaker, The Futureheads, Gyroscope and Starsailor.

More about Sean +

Alongside his studio career, Sean has played guitar with Richard Ashcroft, Roger Daltrey and The Proclaimers, and was a founding member of the critically acclaimed band Moke. His career has always resisted being confined to one genre or role.

At Rancho, desert-rock collective RedRacer took shape with Sean on lead guitar, alongside longtime collaborator John Hogg, bassist Abby Travis (Beck, Elastica), David Catching (Eagles of Death Metal, Mojave Lords) and Joey Castillo (Queens of the Stone Age, Danzig), among others from the Joshua Tree scene.

Together with longtime musical collaborator Jesse Wood, Sean has launched ReKognition Sound, an independent label and creative platform that takes his work beyond traditional record production.

During 2026, he has also expanded into film, television and gaming, working on projects for clients including Netflix and Raw Power Games, alongside songwriting projects and writing sessions for UMG and BMG at Metropolis.
