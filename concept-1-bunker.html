// Our Story

FIVE YEARS.
ONE VISION.

"A labour of love to transform a longstanding piece of Brighton history into something extraordinary."

Salvation Studios didn't happen overnight. It was built room by room, decision by decision — the obsessive result of five years of renovation, curation, and dedication to the craft of recorded sound.

Housed in a building with deep roots in Brighton's cultural history, every corner of this facility was designed with one question in mind: what does a musician actually need to make their best work?

The answer is here. Come find it.
