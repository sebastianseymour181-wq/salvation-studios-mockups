What animation intensity feels right?

All three use Lenis + GSAP. The difference is how dramatically content moves.

A — Subtle & Polished

Smooth scrolling, soft fade-ups, gentle parallax. Content breathes. Nothing shouts. Apple-level restraint.

B — Cinematic & Bold

Dramatic text reveals, pinned sections that hold while content scrolls through, strong parallax on hero. Feels like a film studio site.

C — Immersive

Everything reacts. Mouse parallax on the hero, horizontal scroll sections, scaling images, staggered word-by-word reveals. Maximum impact.
