Writing Rooms

Space to Think

Two dedicated creative suites packed with vintage synths, instruments, and production gear — built for writers, producers, and composers.

The Subterranean Room

WR–01: Underground

The Subterranean Writing Room is a creative world unto itself — tucked away from the main studio, it's a space that encourages experimentation and deep focus. Packed with vintage synthesisers, drum machines, and production hardware alongside a full DAW setup, it's the ideal environment for producers and writers looking for a creative catalyst.

Vintage Synths Drum Machines Pro Tools Logic Pro
The Mezzanine Room

WR–02: Elevated

The Mezzanine Writing Room sits above the main studio, offering an elevated perspective — literally and creatively. An open, light-filled space for producers and writers who need room to think and the freedom to work loud. Full DAW setup, a selection of keyboards and instruments, and a direct line to the main control room if you want to take ideas straight to tape.
