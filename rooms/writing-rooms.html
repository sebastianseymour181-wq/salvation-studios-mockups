Writing Rooms

Flexible and collaborative writing spaces

Our writing rooms provide an inspiring, relaxed and creative atmosphere for all types of writing sessions from large camps to small writing teams.

Subterranean Room

Subterranean Room

Our underground lounge and kitchen area features a comfortable sofa, full kitchen, dining table, widescreen television and PlayStation 5 for taking breaks during long studio days. It can also become an inspiring writing room when equipped with our Universal Audio Apollo interfaces, MIDI keyboards and active Genelec monitors and is great for accommodating groups of songwriters for collaborative writing sessions.

Mezzanine Room

Mezzanine Room

A comfortable space with plenty of natural light and a view of the stained-glass window. A perfect space for group writing sessions, the Mezzanine room can be equipped with Universal Audio Apollo interfaces, MIDI keyboard, microphones and active Genelec monitors for collaborative writing sessions.

The Bunker

The Bunker

The Bunker is also available as a writing room for artists, producers and small teams who want a focused production suite with synths, keys, effects, outboard and a 54 Series Neve Console close at hand.
