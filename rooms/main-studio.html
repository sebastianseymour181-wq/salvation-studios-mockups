The Main Studio

The heart of Salvation.

The main studio is made up of a control room housing our 70's Neve 8068 console and ATC monitoring, as well as a spacious live room surrounded by a vocal booth, two glass-fronted isolation booths and a dedicated amp booth. The main studio is perfect for full-band live tracking, drum recording, overdubs, filmed live sessions and strings recording.
