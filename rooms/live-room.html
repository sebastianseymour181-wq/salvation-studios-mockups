01 — Primary Tracking Space

The Live Room

The finest tracking room in the South of England. A 13-metre vaulted ceiling, natural light, and John Flynn acoustics.

The Space

Built for the ages

Salvation Studios' Live Room is the result of five years of renovation — a former Salvation Army concert hall transformed into a world-class recording facility. World-renowned acoustician and architect John Flynn was given carte blanche to design every element of the space, resulting in a room with extraordinary acoustic character. A 13-metre vaulted ceiling provides natural air and reverb bloom, a fully floated floor system eliminates external vibration, and retractable glass-fronted isolation booths allow for simultaneous tracking.

Natural light floods the room through the original stained-glass window. There is simply nowhere else like it in the South of England — and very few rooms anywhere in the world that match it for character, size, and acoustic quality.
