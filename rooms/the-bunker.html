The Bunker

Writing rooms beneath the main studio

A focused creative wing for writing, production and longer studio days, with the Mezzanine Room and Subterranean Room grouped under one clear Bunker route.

Bunker overview

A self-contained creative zone.

The Bunker is Salvation’s dedicated production, writing and mixing studio, combining a vintage Neve 54-series console, boutique analogue equipment, and a modern hybrid workflow. Designed for writing, production, overdubs, and mixing, it offers artists and producers access to high-end analogue gear within an intimate, creative environment, complemented by a curated collection of rare synthesizers, keyboards as well as an upright piano.

Subterranean Room

A 9.1m × 4.2m underground room with comfortable sofa, full kitchen, dining table, widescreen television and PlayStation 5 for breaks. It can also become an inspiring writing room with desk, Universal Audio Apollo interface, MIDI keyboard and active Genelec monitors.

Recording system
