Iso Booths &

Simultaneous tracking for multiple musicians, plus a dedicated space to crank guitar and bass amps without compromise.

Simultaneous Tracking

Two Independent Iso Booths

The Live Room's two fully retractable, independently floated, glass-fronted isolation booths allow multiple musicians to perform together simultaneously — each hearing one another perfectly, while retaining total sonic isolation for the recording. This is the setup that defines how great records have always been made: musicians in the same room, feeding off each other's energy, but captured with complete clarity.

Amplification

The Amp Booth

Our dedicated amp booth allows guitarists and bassists to track at real amplifier volumes without compromise — crank your Marshall or Fender to the sweet spot and let the room do the rest. The booth is fully isolated from the live room so loud amp tracking never bleeds into other performances. Full line of sight to both the control room and the live room.
