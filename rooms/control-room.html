02 — Mixing & Engineering

The Control Room

Home to our fully restored 1973 Neve 8068 in a room designed by John Flynn. The finest mixing environment in the South of England.

The Environment

Designed for precision

The Control Room at Salvation Studios was designed by John Flynn — the acoustician who built the control rooms at both Olympic and Abbey Road Studios. Working alongside the studio's founders, Flynn created an environment of surgical acoustic precision, optimised for the Neve 8068 console and ATC soffit-mounted monitors. The result is a room you can trust completely — where what you hear is what the world will hear.

The Console

The Neve 8068

Fully restored and immaculately maintained, our 1973 Neve 8068 is one of the finest examples of its kind in the world. Every module has been serviced and recapped to perform at its peak. The Neve 8068 defines the sonic character of this room — warm, musical, and utterly transparent in the ways that matter. 'We pushed up the faders on the immaculate Neve 8068 and it was all there.' — Mike Horner.
