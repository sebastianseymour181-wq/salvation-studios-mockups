Recording Studio Brighton

A Brighton recording studio built for serious records

Record, mix, write and build whole-project sessions inside Salvation's restored 1910 hall: a 13m vaulted live room, 1973 Neve 8068 console, ATC monitoring, rare microphones and producer/engineer support.

Why artists book Salvation

Not a rehearsal room. A complete record-making complex.

Salvation Studios serves artists, bands, producers, labels and managers who need more than a spare-room vocal booth. The complex combines a dramatic live room, accurate control room, writing spaces, isolation, backline, rare outboard and a practical Brighton location moments from the seafront.

Use the studio for full-band tracking, drum recording, vocal production, mixing, dry hire, producer-led sessions or multi-room writing camps.
