Whole complex overview

Our spaces

A restored 1910 hall built around a dramatic live room, trusted control room, isolation, vocal capture and a self-contained Bunker writing wing.

The whole space

A complete record-making complex.

Salvation is arranged so writing, tracking, overdubbing and mixing can happen without breaking momentum. The main hall gives bands and drums scale; the control room gives engineers the Neve/ATC reference point; booths keep simultaneous recording flexible; The Bunker gives writers and producers a private production base.
