Our Heritage

Five years.
One vision.

Salvation Studios is the result of five obsessive years of renovation — a longstanding piece of Brighton history transformed room by room into a world-class recording facility.

"A labour of love that turned a historic Brighton building into something truly extraordinary."

Every acoustic decision, every piece of outboard gear, every square foot of this space was chosen with one person in mind: the musician who knows exactly what they need.
