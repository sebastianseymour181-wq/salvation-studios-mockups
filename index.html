Our Story

A labour
of love

Built inside a restored 1910 Salvation Army hall, Salvation is designed from the ground up for sound. The space combines world-class acoustics, carefully curated vintage equipment and an inspiring and creative atmosphere.

Salvation Studios is the result of five obsessive years of renovation — a longstanding piece of Brighton history transformed into a world-class recording facility.

Designed by world-renowned acoustician and architect John Flynn — the man behind the control rooms of both Olympic and Abbey Road Studios — every room was built from the ground up with one purpose: to help musicians capture the sound in their heads.

The result is a beautifully finished, massive-sounding, and deeply inspiring place to make music.

Isolation & Amp Booths

The Main Studio features a range of dedicated recording spaces, including two large isolation booths, a vocal booth, and a dedicated amp booth. The Bunker

The Bunker is Salvation’s dedicated production, writing and mixing studio, combining a vintage Neve 54-series console and boutique analogue equipment. Designed for writing, production, overdubs, and mixing, it offers access to high-end analogue gear within an intimate, creative environment, complemented by a curated collection of rare synthesizers and keyboards as well as an upright piano.

What We Do

From tracking live bands to finishing mixes, Salvation offers a full range of professional studio services.

Recording Mixing Mastering Live Videos Signature Sessions Giveaways & Offers Accommodation and Hospitality
Recording

Record your music in an acoustically exceptional environment with access to the best microphones, analogue outboard and an extensive collection of vintage instruments.
Mixing

Bring clarity, depth and balance to your recordings in our precisely tuned listening environment for professional sounding mixes.
Mastering

Give your music the final polish with professional mastering. Carried out off-site in a dedicated mastering suite, our engineer uses specialist, state-of-the-art mastering equipment for the best possible translation.
Live Videos

Cut through the noise and capture your performance with high-quality live video sessions recorded in our beautiful studio space, combining exceptional sound with professional videography.
Signature Sessions

A unique opportunity to collaborate with elite producers in a world-class studio environment. Signature Sessions brings together artists and top-tier producers to create records at the highest level.
Catering and accommodation

Our team can handle any hospitality or logistical requests, such as accommodation and catering, allowing you more time to focus on the music.
