Our Story

A labour
of love

Salvation Studios is the result of five obsessive years of renovation — a longstanding piece of Brighton history transformed into a world-class recording facility.

Designed by world-renowned acoustician and architect John Flynn — the man behind the control rooms of both Olympic and Abbey Road Studios — every room was built from the ground up with one purpose: to help musicians capture the sound in their heads.

The result is a beautifully finished, massive-sounding, and deeply inspiring place to make music.
