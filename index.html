Our Story

A labour
of love

Salvation Studios is the result of five obsessive years of renovation — a longstanding piece of Brighton history transformed into a world-class recording facility.

Designed by world-renowned acoustician and architect John Flynn — the man behind the control rooms of both Olympic and Abbey Road Studios — every room was built from the ground up with one purpose: to help musicians capture the sound in their heads.

The result is a beautifully finished, massive-sounding, and deeply inspiring place to make music.

01 — Primary Tracking Space
The Live Room

World-renowned acoustician John Flynn was given carte blanche to transform the old Salvation Army Concert Hall into a world-class tracking room. A 13-metre vaulted ceiling, natural light, a stained-glass window, a fully floated floor system, and retractable isolation booths — this is somewhere truly special.

Control Room

What We Offer

Recording

Full-service recording sessions with in-house producers and engineers. From first note to final print — in rooms that have been trusted by the world's leading artists.
Mixing

Mix on our fully restored 1973 Neve 8068 in a John Flynn–designed room with ATC soffit-mounted monitors. The finest mixing environment in the South of England.
Dry Hire

Bring your own team and book our rooms by the hour with full access to all in-house equipment and facilities. Flexible and transparent.
Writing Rooms

Two dedicated creative suites — the Subterranean Room and the Mezzanine Room — stocked with vintage synths, keys, and production gear. Perfect for writing camps and sessions.
Lighting & Production

State-of-the-art lighting throughout, infinitely configurable for any colour and intensity. Create the perfect atmosphere for every session, every time.
Catering & Hospitality

Long sessions demand great food. Our in-house catering and hospitality service keeps you fuelled and in the zone from start to finish.
Recording Studio Brighton

Built for artists searching for a real studio in Brighton.

Salvation is a world-class recording, mixing and writing complex in Brighton and Hove: a restored 1910 hall with a 13m live room, restored 1973 Neve 8068 console, ATC monitoring, writing rooms, isolation, vintage outboard and rare backline.
