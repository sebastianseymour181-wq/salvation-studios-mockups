A five-year labour of love transforming Brighton history into a state-of-the-art recording facility.

Salvation Studios wasn't built quickly. It was built right. Five years of obsessive renovation, room-by-room acoustic treatment, and hand-picked equipment selections.

Every decision was made with a single type of person in mind: the musician who knows what they want and needs a space that can deliver it without compromise.

Brighton has always been a city that takes music seriously. We're the space that matches that ambition.
