Our Engineers

Meet the Engineers

More than just a recording studio

Every session booked at Salvation comes with one of our talented engineers. From the moment you arrive, you’ll have an experienced professional in the room who understands the technical side of recording and, just as importantly, understands how to help you get the best out of your session.

Experience in the room

Knowledge that makes the most of every session.

Our team of recording, mixing and mastering engineers have worked across an incredible range of artists and projects, including Wet Leg, Amyl and The Sniffers, alt-J, Myles Smith, David Gilmour, shame, Stereophonics, Lana Del Rey, Judas Priest, Ed Sheeran, Jeff Beck, Enter Shikari, Skindred, JADE, JP Cooper and many many more.

That experience means you’re not simply booking a room and a collection of equipment - you’re booking the knowledge and expertise of an engineer who knows how to make the most of both.

Before your booking

Your session, your plan.

We believe the best sessions start well before you walk through the door.

Well in advance of your booking, we’ll put you in touch directly with the engineer who will be working with you. This gives you the opportunity to discuss your plans, the setup you need, your tracks, equipment requirements and anything else that will help make the day run as smoothly as possible.

By the time you arrive at Salvation, your engineer will already have a clear understanding of what you want to achieve, allowing you to get straight into the creative process.

Experience you can rely on

Focus on making the music.

Whether you’re tracking a live band, recording vocals, producing a new project, mixing a finished record or working towards a master, our engineers bring real-world experience from some of the biggest and most exciting names in music.

We’re here to make sure the technical side of your session is taken care of - so you can focus on making the music.
