Salvation Studios

A space that inspires great records.

Salvation isn't just another recording studio.

Built inside a beautifully restored 1910 hall, Salvation is a unique creative space designed to help artists achieve the very best from their music.

With exceptional acoustics, world-class equipment and inspiring surroundings, we offer an environment where creativity comes first.

Whether you're recording an album, developing a new sound or bringing a project to life, Salvation provides the space, equipment and expertise to make it happen.

01 /

Exceptional Acoustics

Designed by renowned acoustician John Flynn, our spaces offer exceptional recording and listening environments.

02 /

World-Class Equipment

Featuring a vintage Neve console, exceptional analogue outboard and an extensive microphone collection.

03 /

An Inspiring Environment

A unique combination of historic architecture, carefully designed studios and creative spaces.
